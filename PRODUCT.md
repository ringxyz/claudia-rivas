# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, business partners, podcast guests, fashion collaborators, and media contacts discovering Claudia Rivas from mobile or desktop. They need to understand her work quickly, see credible visual proof, and contact her without friction.

## Product Purpose

Present Claudia Rivas as a single personal brand across entrepreneurship, fashion, and Claud 360. The landing page should build confidence, make each facet easy to explore, and convert relevant interest into direct contact.

## Brand Personality

Poised, precise, cinematic. The experience should feel confident, calm, and personal.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates, decorative luxury clichés, dense corporate layouts, visual effects that compete with Claudia, and low-quality media treatment. Apple-style clarity is a reference for restraint and pacing, not for imitation.

## Design Principles

- Let Claudia's photography and motion carry the story.
- Use space, scale, and pacing to create confidence.
- Keep navigation and contact actions immediate at every viewport.
- Make motion feel smooth and purposeful, with an equivalent reduced-motion experience.
- Preserve image fidelity while keeping the first visit fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful alternative text, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and responsive layouts from small phones to large desktop displays.
